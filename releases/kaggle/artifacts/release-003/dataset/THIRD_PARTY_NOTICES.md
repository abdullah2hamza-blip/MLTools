Original MLTools code and research annotations/results are Apache-2.0. This
release is a curated projection; it does not license every file in the private
research repository. Component terms prevail for third-party files.

The exact original manifests, README cards, license texts and byte/revision
identities are included under `research/publication/selection/`. Prepared
statistics/configuration leaves under `research/publication/prepared/` are
research derivatives: the numeric control preserves source bytes; offset,
slope and composed changes are declared in `plan.json`, including each output
hash and changed value. No learned model weights are distributed or executed.

LeRobot, OpenPI, Robotruth and Policy Space implementations are referenced by
immutable source identities in the saved observations and integration ledgers.
Their installed packages/checkouts are not redistributed here. Their original
project URLs and source pins remain available in the payload. A source/hash
assertion from a producer is not an attestation of the upstream runtime.

TeX styles and compiler material are omitted. In particular, TMLR's Apache-2.0
license does not replace the distinct LPPL terms embedded in fancyhdr.sty and
tmlr.bst. Unresolved OpenPI GCS material and Tether source are excluded.

The dataset license describes the original authored contribution, subject to
these retained third-party notices. It conveys no trademark endorsement,
physical safety assurance or additional patent/weight rights from publishers.
